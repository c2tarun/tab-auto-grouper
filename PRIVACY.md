# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-14_

Tab Auto Grouper does not collect, store, sell, or share any user data.

- The extension reads the URL and opener of newly opened tabs only to decide which tab group they belong to and to name that group after the source site. This happens entirely in your browser.
- The only thing it stores is a list of tab group IDs it created, kept in `chrome.storage.session`. It is cleared when the browser closes and is never synced.
- The extension makes no network requests and includes no analytics or third-party code.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/c2tarun/tab-auto-grouper/issues
